What is Jiu Jitsu?

Brazilian Jiu Jitsu is a grappling martial art built on control. Standing or on the ground, the goal is to take a dominant position and finish with a submission. Leverage and technique do the work, not size or strength, which is why a smaller person can handle a bigger one.

On the mat, you learn to stay calm under pressure and find solutions in real time. Kids build focus, patience, and confidence. Adults build fitness, resilience, and a clearer mind. Everyone builds it the same way: class after class.

All levels welcome. Kids and adults.

Adult classes

Our adult classes follow a structured program, so every session builds on the last. You'll learn the fundamentals of jiu jitsu, from positions and escapes to submissions and self defense.

Through detail, repetition, and sparring, you'll develop the timing and control that make it work. Beginners start with the basics, and experienced students keep sharpening theirs.

Your child will learn more than Jiu Jitsu.

At Zaandam Jiu Jitsu Club, we believe the mat is a place where children discover who they are. Through jiu jitsu, they learn to move, to think, and to solve problems under pressure.

Without realising it, they are also learning to persist, to trust themselves, and to handle hard things with calm.

What they build here, they carry everywhere, into school, into friendships, into life. Not because we tell them to. Because they lived it every class, every week.

Who is Danillo Bighi?

Danillo was born and raised in Rio de Janeiro, the birthplace of Brazilian Jiu Jitsu. He has been training since 2004 and received his black belt from Roger Gracie, the greatest jiu jitsu competitor of all time.

A coach and team leader with over two decades on the mat, Danillo has taught students of all ages and levels.

The son of two teachers, Danillo grew up understanding the value of good instruction. He has led strong adult and children's programmes in both Rio de Janeiro and Amsterdam, and his focus is now fully in Zaandam.
